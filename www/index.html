Who to screen for type 2 diabetes

ADA criteria for asymptomatic adults.

  • All adults ≥35 years; repeat every 3 years if normal.
  • Any age, BMI ≥25 kg/m² (≥23 Asian ancestry) plus ≥1 risk factor: first-degree relative with diabetes, CVD, hypertension, HDL <35 or TG >250 mg/dL, PCOS, inactivity, acanthosis nigricans, MASLD.
  • Prediabetes: yearly. Previous GDM: at least every 3 years for life.
  • HIV, antipsychotics, long-term glucocorticoids.
  • Youth: overweight, after puberty or age ≥10, plus ≥1 risk factor.
